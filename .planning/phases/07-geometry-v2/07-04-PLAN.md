Word-to-branch assignment per D-13:
- Proportion = branch.sdf_volume / total_sdf_volume
- Words sorted by weight descending first, then assigned to branches in proportion
- Lexicographic tiebreak on branch_id to maintain determinism (D-15/Pitfall 4)

Sub-SDF per branch (D-12):
- sub_sdf = np.where(mat_result.branch_map == branch_id, sdf, 0.0)
- sub_mask = (mat_result.branch_map == branch_id) & mask
